This file names the people responsible for netresearch/timetracker. It is the human-readable counterpart to [`.github/CODEOWNERS`](.github/CODEOWNERS); keep the two in sync.

The project is developed at [Netresearch DTT GmbH](https://www.netresearch.de/). Repository administration (settings, branch protection, secrets) rests with the GitHub organisation owners of [netresearch](https://github.com/netresearch).

## What a maintainer does

- Reviews and merges pull requests, and is accountable for what lands on `main`.
- Cuts releases and publishes the release notes (see [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md), section *Releases*).
- Triages security reports and drives them to a fix, under the timelines in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
- Triages incoming issues and keeps the roadmap in [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md) current.
- Maintains the CI, supply-chain and dependency configuration.

## Reaching a maintainer

- Bugs and feature requests: [GitHub issues](https://github.com/netresearch/timetracker/issues).
- Security reports: **never** as a public issue — use [private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/netresearch/timetracker/security/advisories/new), see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
- Everything else: [GitHub discussions](https://github.com/netresearch/timetracker/discussions) or a comment on the relevant issue.

## Becoming a maintainer

The project currently has one maintainer. Contributors who submit sustained, reviewed work may be invited to join; the decision rests with the current maintainers and the organisation owners. The criteria and the succession arrangement are described in [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md).
